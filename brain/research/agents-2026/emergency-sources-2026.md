## 10. INTEGRATION NOTES FOR NOMAD HYPER

1. **Format normalization**: Convert all PDFs → searchable PDF (OCR) + plain-text extraction for low-bandwidth/low-battery reading.
2. **Chunking**: Split large manuals (FM 21-76, USDA Canning) into chapter-level files for selective sync.
3. **Metadata**: Embed source URL, retrieval date, SHA-256 hash, license tag in each file's front matter.
4. **Verification**: Cross-check medical/safety content against current CDC/USDA/DoD guidance before relying on older editions.
5. **Attribution**: All US Gov works are PD — no attribution legally required, but best practice to cite source agency + pub number.
6. **Update cadence**: FEMA/CDC/USDA update annually; Army manuals every 3–5 years. Schedule automated re-fetch.
7. **Storage budget**: Tier 1 = ~200 MB; Tier 1+2 = ~600 MB; Full Tier 1-3 = ~4–5 GB (with Kiwix ZIMs).
